# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately. Send a direct message on [Discord](https://discord.gg/nomnom-762409407488720918) to any member with the **Developer** role.

If you cannot reach a Developer on Discord, contact me on Reddit: `u/zencq`.

Please do not report vulnerabilities in public issues, discussions, or other public channels.

Include as much of the following as you can:

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept
- The affected versions
- Any relevant configuration or environment details

Do not include personal or other sensitive information in your report. Remove or redact credentials, tokens, private user data, and unrelated personal details from examples, logs, and proof-of-concept materials.

## Response and Disclosure

We will make a best effort to acknowledge reports and work to resolve valid vulnerabilities promptly. We do not guarantee a specific response or resolution timeframe.

Please allow us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and release a fix before sharing vulnerability details publicly. We ask that vulnerabilities not be publicly disclosed until a fix is available. We will coordinate with the reporter on disclosure after the issue is fixed.
